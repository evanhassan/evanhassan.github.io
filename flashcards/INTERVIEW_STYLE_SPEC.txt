CUSTOM FLASHCARD DESIGN STANDARD

ROLE
Act like an investment banker reviewing the user's actual Olin / Huntsman
public-to-public merger model. Questions must test whether the builder understands
the mechanics behind the workbook rather than whether a formula was memorized.

QUESTION STYLE
- Frame the card as a new problem or model-review situation.
- Use prompts such as:
  "You open the workbook and see..."
  "An associate challenges..."
  "A VP asks you to defend..."
  "What breaks if..."
  "How would you audit..."
- Tie model-review cards to actual workbook choices, links, signs, schedules,
  assumptions, and bugs.
- Ask one clear question per card.
- Avoid generic definitions unless embedded in a practical scenario.
- Mix accounting walks, formula diagnostics, assumption challenges, what-if
  changes, audit questions, and transaction-structure questions.

ANSWER CHOICES
- The correct answer must be concise, technically complete, and defensible.
- Wrong answers must be plausible near-misses, not random statements from
  unrelated topics.
- Common distractor patterns:
  wrong sign;
  correct account but wrong cash-flow section;
  point-in-time shares instead of weighted-average shares;
  net presentation combined with a second contra-account deduction;
  balance-sheet reclassification confused with cash repayment;
  accounting expense confused with cash payment;
  target refinancing confused with new debt issuance;
  economic dilution confused with GAAP anti-dilution.
- Avoid answer choices that are obviously absurd.
- Keep custom options short enough to display in full.

FEEDBACK
- Explain the mechanical reason the correct answer works.
- Distinguish gross debt from carrying value, gross PP&E from net PP&E,
  reclassification from cash transaction, expense from cash payment, EBITDA
  from embedded depreciation, and refinancing from "nothing happened."
- Use small numerical examples when they clarify the linkage.

TOPICS
- Three statements and balance-sheet checks
- Working capital and cash-flow signs
- D&A, capex, PP&E, intangibles, and asset sales
- Debt maturity, current portion, interest, refinancing, issuance costs,
  custom activity, and revolver circularity
- Share roll-forwards, weighted-average and diluted shares, SBC, APIC,
  Common Stock, Treasury Stock, dividends, and retained earnings
- Sources & Uses, stock/cash/debt consideration, target and acquirer debt,
  transaction fees, financing fees, and dynamic VBA
- PPA, goodwill, DTLs, synergies, opening pro forma balance sheet,
  consolidation, accretion/dilution, and credit metrics
- Formula auditing, copied links, exact source reconciliation, and macros

DIFFICULTY
Start with fundamental mechanics, then demand precise language and complete
three-statement or transaction explanations. Do not reward vague statements
such as "nothing happened" when gross borrowing and repayment both occurred.

M&I 400 REAL
The M&I 400 Real deck remains source-derived and was not rewritten under this
custom-card standard.
