6. PRO FORMA BALANCE-SHEET PROJECTION
======================================================================

The forecast projects FROM the Pro Forma Opening Balance Sheet.

Correct architecture for ordinary operating accounts:
Ending Pro Forma Balance
=
Prior Pro Forma Balance
+ Olin annual movement
+ Huntsman annual movement
+ any account-specific transaction / PPA movement

For the first pro forma forecast year:
2027 Ending Balance
=
12/31/26 Pro Forma Opening Balance
+ 2027 Olin movement
+ 2027 Huntsman movement
+ 2027 PPA / debt / equity movements

For later years:
Current-Year Ending Balance
=
Prior-Year Pro Forma Ending Balance
+ current-year combined movements

Why:
- The opening balance may include transaction adjustments, eliminations, and reclassifications.
- Directly replacing it with Olin current-year balance + Huntsman current-year balance can erase those adjustments.
- Direct addition is equivalent only for accounts with no opening adjustment.

Account-specific treatment:

Cash:
- same-year CFS Ending Cash is the source of truth

Operating current accounts:
- roll from the opening balance using combined annual changes

Gross PP&E:
- prior Gross PP&E
- minus negative capex
- plus disposals / other movements

Accumulated Depreciation:
- prior negative balance
- minus current depreciation
- plus disposal / other adjustments

Goodwill:
- hold constant absent impairment or another acquisition

Intangible Assets:
- opening acquired intangibles
- less cumulative new amortization
- plus any supported additions

Deferred Taxes:
- unwind the incremental PPA DTL through the book-versus-cash tax schedule

Debt:
- roll from the opening pro forma debt balances using the detailed debt schedules
- current versus long-term classification is noncash
- principal issuance / repayment drives financing cash flow

Retained Earnings:
- prior retained earnings
+ parent-attributable net income
+ signed dividends
+ any explicit direct adjustment

Common Shares Outstanding:
- Olin ending shares
+ full consideration shares after close

10. PRO FORMA CASH FLOW STATEMENT
======================================================================

Cash Flow from Operations:
Net Income
+ D&A
+ Stock-Based Compensation
+ Debt Issuance Cost Amortization
+ Change in Working Capital
- Gain on Asset Sales
+ Change in Other Operating Assets & Liabilities
= CFO

Operating NWC:
Operating Current Assets excluding Cash
- Operating Current Liabilities excluding Debt

Change in Working Capital convention:
Prior-Year ONWC
- Current-Year ONWC

Cash Flow from Investing:
- Capital Expenditures
+ Investments, Net
+ Proceeds from Asset Sales
+ Acquisitions
+ Other Investing Activities
= CFI

Cash Flow from Financing:
+ Change in Short-Term Debt
+ Debt Issuance / Repayment
+ Stock Issuance / Repurchases
+ Dividends Paid
= CFF

Net Change in Cash:
= CFO + CFI + CFF + FX Effects

Ending Cash:
= Beginning Cash + Net Change in Cash

The balance-sheet cash line links to same-year Ending Cash.

Free Cash Flow:
= CFO + Capital Expenditures
because Capex is stored as a negative number

Net Debt:
= Gross Debt - Cash

Net Leverage:
= Net Debt / Normalized EBITDA

12. NET DEBT INTERPRETATION
======================================================================

The modeled net-debt path is not a solvency concern.

It shows:
- rapid deleveraging;
- net cash by 2030;
- approximately $3.1bn of net cash by 2031.

The issue is model realism:
- Free cash flow is large.
- Scheduled debt is repaid.
- Dividends remain modest.
- No buybacks are modeled.
- No acquisitions are modeled.
- No explicit excess-cash deployment is modeled.

Therefore cash accumulates indefinitely.

Final assumption required:
Choose one of the following as the explicit capital-allocation policy:
- maintain unallocated excess cash;
- additional debt repayment;
- share repurchases;
- higher dividends;
- acquisitions / strategic investment;
- a combination.

Do not manipulate Free Cash Flow or debt merely to avoid negative net debt.
